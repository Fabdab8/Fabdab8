
FabDab EarthFi  
**Empowering rural artisans and communities through culture, climate, and crypto - built on Solana.**

##  Overview  
FabDab EarthFi is a **decentralized social impact platform** designed to connect rural artists, artisans, and women-led communities to the global Web3 economy.  
By integrating **Solana Pay** and **NFT tooling**, FabDab EarthFi allows users to **mint cultural NFTs, receive direct payments, and participate in climate-positive initiatives** through a transparent DAO model.

Our mission is to **bridge traditional craftsmanship and sustainable innovation**, using blockchain to build an economy rooted in culture, community, and the Earth.


##  Features  
-  **NFT Minting Portal** - Onboard artisans to create and sell digital representations of their art.  
-  **Solana Pay Integration** – Fast, low-cost global payments for crafts, donations, and services.  
-  **EarthFi DAO** – Community-governed fund rewarding eco-actions and cultural impact.  
-  **Onboarding & Education** – Web3 training for rural creators to help them build financial independence.  
-  **Cultural Marketplace** – Trade artisan goods, NFTs, and eco-products in one decentralized hub.  


##  Problem  
Rural artists and artisans often lack access to global markets and modern payment systems.  
They face barriers in visibility, fair compensation, and digital literacy.  

**FabDab EarthFi** leverages Solana’s speed, low fees, and open infrastructure to solve this — creating inclusion, visibility, and sustainable income streams.


##  Architecture  
- **Frontend:** React + Tailwind + Solana Wallet Adapter  
- **Blockchain:** Solana (Candy Machine / Metaplex SDK)  
- **Payments:** Solana Pay  
- **Database / Storage:** Arweave or IPFS for NFT metadata  
- **Governance:** FabDab DAO (using SPL governance & token-based voting)
